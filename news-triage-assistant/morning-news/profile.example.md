# Morning news profile — example

Copy to `profile.local.md` and rewrite for yourself. That file is gitignored;
this one is the published template.

The digest is only as good as this file. `prompt.md` deliberately contains no
opinion about what matters — it reads whatever you write here, so a vague profile
produces a vague digest. Be specific about what you want *and* what you don't.

## Interest profile (priority order)

1. **AI & agent infrastructure** — model releases, agent tooling, the companies
   building them, and the regulation landing on them.
2. **Tech & software** — engineering tools, developer platforms, cybersecurity.
3. **Business** — company news, markets, M&A, earnings, especially where they
   intersect the above.
4. **Science & health research** — findings with a real result, not press
   releases.
5. **Local** — civic news, transit, weather, schools for your city.
6. **Politics, lightly** — the top few national and international stories. Not
   daily minutiae.

## Themes

| id | Heading | Items | What belongs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 🤖 AI | 5–8 | Model releases, agent products, AI companies, deals, lawsuits, regulation. |
| B | 💻 Tech & Software | 5–8 | Engineering tools, developer platforms, security, big-tech product news. |
| C | 📈 Business | 5–8 | Company news, markets, M&A, earnings. |
| D | 🧬 Science & Health | 3–6 | Research findings, public-health results, biotech. |
| E | 🌴 Local | 3–6 | City civic news, transit, weather, schools. |
| F | 🇺🇸 National | 3 | National politics. |
| G | 🌍 International | 3 | World news. |
| H | ✍️ Opinion | 3–5 | Op-eds worth the time — `section` is `Opinion`, or the URL contains `/opinion/`. |

Target ~25–35 items total across A–D.

**Posting order:** A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H

**Short form** (one factual sentence + link, no bold headline): F, G.

**Light-news threshold:** fewer than 15 items across A–D.

## Classification rules

Write the distinctions you keep having to make. The ones that earn their place
are between two themes that genuinely overlap — without a written rule the same
story lands in a different bucket every morning. For example:

- **Science & Health (D) vs Business (C).** A study result is D. A biotech
  company's funding round is C. When it is framed as a business story, prefer C.
- **Opinion (H) is op-eds only.** A news story with a slight slant stays in its
  primary theme.

## Drop aggressively

Name the categories you never want to see. Being explicit here does more for
digest quality than any amount of tuning elsewhere — for example: routine sports,
tabloid clickbait, local crime unless nationally significant, stock-price
minutiae, horse-race political coverage.
